Privacy Policy – Havruta

Havruta is an application that allows users to create and participate in personal goal competitions with friends.

Information We Collect

  • Email address (for authentication)
  • Username
  • User-generated content (competitions, progress updates)
  • Device information (for notifications via Firebase)

Signing In with Google or Facebook

If you choose to sign in with Google or Facebook, that service shares your name, email address and profile picture with Havruta, and a unique account identifier that lets you sign in again. We use your email address to sign you in and to let friends invite you, and your name only to suggest a username, which you can change. Your name and profile picture link are kept with your sign-in record in Firebase Authentication; Havruta doesn't display or otherwise use the picture. We never request access to your contacts, posts or friends list, and never post anything on your behalf.

You can connect or stop using these sign-in methods at any time. To disconnect Havruta from your Google or Facebook account, remove it in that account's settings (for example, Google Account → Security → Third-party apps, or Facebook → Settings → Apps and Websites).

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To enable competitions and progress tracking
  • To send push notifications related to user activity

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties, except as required to provide the service (e.g., Firebase by Google).

Data Security

We use industry-standard tools and services (Firebase) to store and protect user data.

User Control

Users can stop using the app at any time. To have your account and all associated data deleted — including data received through Google or Facebook sign-in — submit a request on our account deletion page.
